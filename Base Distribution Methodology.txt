College Base Distribution Methodology - Public Schools
	1. Group Quarter from Census Block  --> TAZ Group Quarter (A) assign to closest Campus (closest)
	2. Remove TAZ Group Quarter (A) from Zip Enrollment  [TotEnrolByZipByCollege.csv]
	3. Distribute Remaining Zip Enrollment --> TAZs with HHs based on SL data distribution (fall, weekday, midday)

College Base Distribution Methodology - Private Schools
	1. Group Quarter from Census Block --> TAZ Group Quarter (A) assign to closest Campus (closest)
	2. Remove TAZ Group Quarter (A) from Total Enrollment  [Control Total Spreadsheet]
	3. Distribute Total Enrollment --> TAZs with HHs based on SL data distribution (fall, weekday, midday)
